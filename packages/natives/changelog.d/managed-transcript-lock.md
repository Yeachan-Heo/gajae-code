### Fixed

- On Linux, a full rewrite of a managed session transcript (for example a compaction) and another process's append to the same transcript no longer overlap. Previously an append that landed while the rewrite was being swapped in could be reported saved and yet be missing from the transcript; its bytes survived only in a `.gjc-managed-replace-*` recovery file. Both now hold an exclusive lock on the transcript across their check and their write or swap. A writer that keeps holding the transcript makes the other wait at most about 2 s, after which it fails with the retryable `transcript_busy`. An append whose file changed after it wrote reports `identity_changed_after_write`, distinct from the `identity_mismatch` refusal before writing.
- This covers local filesystems with `flock` and atomic rename exchange (ext4, xfs, btrfs, tmpfs). It does not cover mounts without rename flags or usable `flock` (NFS, 9p such as WSL `/mnt/c`, some FUSE filesystems), which keep the previous unserialized behaviour, or an older gjc that takes no lock.
