### Changed

- A conversation's sidebar context menu now offers `Rename`, which renames that conversation without opening it; the conversation actions menu no longer carries rename.
- `Fork conversation` in the conversation actions menu shows an icon like its neighbours.

### Removed

- The conversation actions menu no longer offers `Model profiles and run settings` or `Full conversation history`; the composer's model controls remain the run-settings surface and the transcript shows the recent history.
