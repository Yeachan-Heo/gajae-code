### Added

- When a terminal already has a conversation open, the app now shows that conversation read-only. It checks before opening the conversation, before each message or other change it makes, and when you select it. Its history stays visible, the composer is replaced by an explanation with a "Check again" button, and the sidebar and header say it is in use in a terminal. While the conversation's own background work is running, the app keeps it open instead, so that work is not ended, and refuses what you send until the work ends or you disconnect it; when the work finishes, the app can still add its result and a reply on its own. Once the terminal closes it, selecting the conversation or "Check again" takes it back.
- The check sees a terminal only while a gjc broker is running (an interactive terminal normally starts one) and answers within half a second; otherwise the app opens or writes the conversation as before. Some cases are not caught by the check: a terminal that opens the conversation just after a check, or while the app's turn is running, is noticed only at the next check, and a turn the app starts by itself when the conversation's background work finishes is not checked. In those cases the shared store still refuses whichever side writes from an older position, so the conversation never splits into branches: when the terminal has written first, the app's message is refused, its text comes back and the app reloads the conversation; when the app has written first, the terminal's write is refused instead.
