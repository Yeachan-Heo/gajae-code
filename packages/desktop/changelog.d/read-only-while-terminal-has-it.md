### Added

- When a terminal already has a conversation open, the app now shows that conversation read-only. It checks before opening the conversation, before each message or other change it makes, and when you select it. Its history stays visible, the composer is replaced by an explanation with a "Check again" button, and the sidebar and header say it is in use in a terminal. While the conversation's own background work is running, the app keeps it open instead, so that work is not ended, and refuses to start anything new in it until the work ends or you disconnect it. Once the terminal closes it, selecting the conversation or "Check again" takes it back.
- The check sees a terminal only while a gjc broker is running (an interactive terminal normally starts one) and answers within half a second; otherwise the app opens or writes the conversation as before. Some cases still end with the conversation split into two branches, with nothing lost: a terminal that opens the conversation just after a check, or while the app's turn is running, is noticed only at the next check, and if the terminal writes first, its turn is already a separate branch; a turn the app starts by itself when the conversation's background work finishes is not checked. When the terminal has written since the app last read the conversation, the app's own write is refused and its text comes back, but a terminal that writes after the app has written starts a separate branch rather than being refused.
