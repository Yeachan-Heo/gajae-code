### Added
- When the app finds a conversation open in a terminal (a gjc process registered with the local broker), it now shows that conversation read-only instead of writing to it too. Its history stays visible, the composer is replaced by an explanation with a "Check again" button, and the sidebar and header say it is in use in a terminal. Once the terminal closes it, selecting the conversation or "Check again" takes it back. When the app cannot check, it opens the conversation as before.
