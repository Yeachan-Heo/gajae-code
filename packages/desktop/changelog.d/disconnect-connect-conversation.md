### Added
- "Disconnect from app" in a conversation's actions menu releases the app's hold on that conversation without deleting it, so the terminal can continue it. A disconnected conversation keeps its history visible read-only, survives restarting the app, and shows a Connect button where the composer was; Connect takes it back and shows anything the terminal added. Disconnecting while a turn or background work is running asks first, then stops the turn as Stop does; the conversation's background jobs, tasks and schedules end with it.
