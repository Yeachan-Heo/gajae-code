### Removed

- SDK embedders: `SessionManager` no longer has the shared-publication methods: `setSessionNameSharingSession()`, `withSharedThinkingControl()`, `contextClearControlEntries()`, `defaultModelSelectionControlEntries()`, and the `append*SharingSession()` family (`appendMessageSharingSession()`, `appendCustomEntrySharingSession()`, `appendCustomMessageEntrySharingSession()`, `appendTemporaryModelChangeSharingSession()`, `appendModelProfileActivationSharingSession()`, `appendServiceTierChangeSharingSession()`, `appendControlEntriesSharingSession()`, `appendCompactionSharingSession()`, `appendThinkingLevelChangeSharingSession()`, `appendModeChangeSharingSession()`, `appendSessionRecapSharingSession()`, `appendToolSelectionsSharingSession()`, `appendTtsrInjectionSharingSession()` and `appendLabelChangeSharingSession()`). The `SharedAppendOutcome`, `SharedEntriesAppendOutcome`, `SharedTitleOutcome`, `SharedControlEntry` and `ModelProfileTranscriptPlan` types and the `SessionManagerTestHooks.beforeSharedAppendPublish` hook are gone too. `SharedControlPublicationError` now only reports `rejected`; its `stale`, `unknown` and `committed_not_applied` codes are gone, and its constructor parameter and public `outcome` field narrowed from `SharedEntriesAppendOutcome` to `{ kind: "rejected"; error }`. Use the ordinary appends (`appendMessage()`, `appendCustomEntry()`, `setSessionName()` and the rest): every session writes its transcript through them.
