### Fixed
- Every history transition (`/new`, `/resume`, `/fork`, branching, handoff, and tree navigation) now saves `!`/`$` command results from the previous turn to the session they were typed in, exactly once, before it switches. Before, a result still being saved through the shared session lease when the switch started could land in the new transcript and the new conversation context, or be lost. A result still waiting to be saved was appended to the next session by `/new`'s first prompt, and dropped without a notice by `/resume`. A result whose own save was already refused keeps its "Could not save" notice and is dropped without a retry. Results queued behind an earlier refusal are still attempted once. While another writer keeps holding the session lease, saving them adds at most one lock-wait budget (about 5 s) per kind of result (`!` and `$`). Results of a kind whose save was refused during the switch are not attempted again. Any result a switch cannot save shows a "Could not save" notice; none is dropped without one.
