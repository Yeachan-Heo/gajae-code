### Added
- Expose identity-fenced read-only profile labels, provider grouping/ranks, availability and role selectors through the public preference service (`await readProfiles()`) so embedders preserve canonical picker presentation without internal imports or catalog refresh. A profile's availability follows the managed `models.profiles.list` decision for the session: its providers are authenticated (directly, through an alternative, or through a configured proxy) and its mapped models resolve. The read only checks that a usable credential exists: it resolves no key, refreshes no OAuth token and records no session credential choice.
